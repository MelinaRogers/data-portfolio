Data pipeline dashboards

Two working ETL pipelines over public metadata, each ending in a self-contained dashboard. Built as portfolio pieces. All figures come from live harvests of open data — nothing is synthetic.

Neither of these is an official system, and neither is affiliated with the organisation whose data it analyses. The first analyses publicly available metadata about Stanford-affiliated research and is modelled on an architecture Stanford University Libraries publishes openly under an Apache-2.0 licence; it is not a Stanford product and carries no Stanford endorsement. The second analyses openly licensed metadata from the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive and is not affiliated with the Internet Archive.

Stanford Research Output / research intelligence

Multi-source harvest, entity resolution across research platforms, a denormalized reporting layer, and a data-quality audit that reports what the numbers cannot cover as well as what they can.

65,552 publications 2023–2025 OpenAlex · Crossref · OpenAPC

Live Music Archive / preservation profile

Preservation and provenance analysis of a digital audio archive: venue and song authority control, setlists parsed from free text, and reconciliation of independently typed transcriptions of the same performance.

18,270 recordings 2,812 performances 1965–1995 Internet Archive
Each dashboard is a single self-contained HTML file — no server, no external requests, no tracking. Source pipelines are not published.
